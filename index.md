## 📂 Applying the Settings
After downloading, you will need to apply the settings in VSCode:

1. Open Visual Studio Code.
2. Go to the menu, and click on `File > Preferences > Settings`.
3. Click on the `Open Settings (JSON)` icon located in the top right corner.
4. Copy the JSON content from the downloaded configuration file.
5. Paste the JSON content into your settings file.
6. Save the changes and restart VSCode.
